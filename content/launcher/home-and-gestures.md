# 👆 Home Screen & Gestures

Learn how to customize your home screen grid, set up handy swipe shortcuts, and manage widgets.

---

## 🖐️ Configurable Gestures

RPDev Launcher supports customizable gestures so you can control your phone faster without stretching your thumb:

| Gesture | Default Action | Available Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Double Tap** | Screen Lock (or Open Dash) | Open Dash, Lock Screen, Open App, Expand Notifications, Search |
| **Swipe Down** | Expand Notification Shade | Notifications, App Drawer, Quick Settings, None |
| **Swipe Up** | Open App Drawer | App Drawer, Global Search, Open Feed |
| **Swipe Left (Page 1)** | Open RPDev Feed Overlay | RPDev Feed (-1 Screen), None |
| **Pinch In** | Home Overview & Page Manager | Page Manager, Wallpapers, Settings |

### How to customize gestures:
1. Long-press on any empty spot on your home screen.
2. Tap **Home settings** &gt; **Gestures**.
3. Tap on the gesture you wish to change (e.g. **Double Tap**).
4. Select your preferred action or choose a specific app to launch.

---

## 📐 Grid Layout & Desktop Customization

You can change how many app icons fit on each screen:

- **Desktop Grid**: Choose from 4x4, 4x5, 5x5, or custom dense grids like 6x6.
- **Icon Sizing**: Adjust icon scale from 80% (compact) to 120% (large).
- **Icon Labels**: Toggle text labels on or off for a minimalist home screen look.
- **Two-Row Dock**: Need more shortcuts at the bottom? Enable two-row dock mode in **Home settings &gt; Dock**.

---

## 🧩 Adding & Resizing Widgets

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets** in the bottom menu.
3. Scroll or search for your favorite app's widget.
4. Touch and hold the widget, then drag it onto your home screen.
5. To resize, long-press the placed widget and drag the round control handles.
