## Key Highlights

- **Pure Performance**: Built directly on AOSP Launcher3 with Android 16 parity (`NexusLauncherRelease`).
- **Recursive Nested Folders**: Organize your apps logically by nesting subfolders as deep as you need.
- **Custom Drawer Folder Icons**: Personalize drawer categories with beautiful icons saved to your device.
- **DataStore Reactive State**: Fast, smooth preference loading that never stutters your animations.
