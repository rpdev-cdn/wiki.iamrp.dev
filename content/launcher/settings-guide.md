# ⚙️ Launcher Settings Walkthrough

No confusing jargon. Here is a complete guide to every setting inside **Home settings** in RPDev Launcher.

---

### 🔍 1. Search &amp; Feed
- **Enable Feed Overlay**: Turns on the swipe-left -1 screen companion.
- **Feed Provider**: Lets you choose which companion app powers your feed. Set this to **RPDev Feed** for the private Google Discover experience.
- **Search Bar on Dock**: Places the search bar at the very bottom of your screen for easy one-handed access.
- **Search Provider**: Choose your default search engine (DuckDuckGo, StartPage, Google, or local app search).

---

### 🎨 2. Theme &amp; Icons
- **Icon Pack**: Apply any icon pack downloaded from the app store or F-Droid.
- **Adaptive Icons**: Forces all older icons into modern circles, squircles, or rounded rectangles for a unified look.
- **Notification Dots**: Displays a subtle dot on app icons when you have unread notifications.

---

### 📐 3. Desktop Grid
- **Columns &amp; Rows**: Change how many icons fit on your screen (e.g. 5 columns by 6 rows).
- **Lock Desktop**: Prevents accidental moving or deleting of icons and widgets.

---

### 📥 4. App Drawer
- **Drawer Sorting**: Sort apps alphabetically, by install date, or by most frequently used.
- **Hidden Apps**: Hide pre-installed bloatware apps you don't use from your drawer without uninstalling them.
- **Category Tabs**: Enable tabs across the top of your drawer to group apps into Work, Social, Games, etc.

---

### ⚡ 5. Gestures &amp; System
- **Double Tap Action**: Choose what happens when you double-tap on an empty spot on your home screen.
- **Restart Launcher**: Quickly reloads the home screen and clears caches if you ever experience visual glitches.
