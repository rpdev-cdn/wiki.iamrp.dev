## 📦 How to Install or Enable Modules

1. In **RPDev Feed**, tap the **Settings icon** &gt; **Hub Modules &amp; Cards**.
2. Built-in modules (like Weather and Battery Telemetry) are active by default.
3. For downloadable catalog modules, tap **Marketplace**, pick your desired module, and tap **Install**.
4. Tap on any module to open its friendly configuration screen.
