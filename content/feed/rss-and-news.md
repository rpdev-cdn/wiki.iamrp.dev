# 📡 RSS Feeds & Reader Mode

Say goodbye to algorithmically manipulated clickbait. With RPDev Feed, you choose the exact websites and blogs you want to read.

---

## ➕ Adding an RSS Feed

1. Open the **RPDev Feed** app.
2. Tap the **Settings gear** &gt; **News Sources &amp; Feeds**.
3. Tap **"Add Feed (+)"**.
4. Enter the URL of any website or RSS feed (e.g. `https://news.ycombinator.com/rss` or `https://github.blog/feed/`).
5. Tap **Save**. The feed will begin syncing automatically!

---

## 📂 Importing from OPML

Already use an RSS reader like Feedly, Inoreader, or NetNewsWire?
1. Export your subscriptions from your current reader as an `.opml` file.
2. In RPDev Feed, tap **Settings &gt; News Sources &gt; Import OPML**.
3. Select your `.opml` file. All your subscriptions will be imported in seconds!

---

## 📖 Distraction-Free Reader Mode

Tapping on any article headline opens our built-in **Offline Reader Mode**:
- Strips away intrusive web advertisements, cookie banners, and pop-ups.
- Formats text in clean, legible typography.
- Saves the full article text so you can finish reading later offline.
