# 🎛️ Customizing & Reordering Cards

Your feed should show what matters to you. RPDev Feed lets you customize the order, appearance, and density of all cards.

---

## ↕️ Reordering Cards

Want Weather at the very top and GitHub at the bottom?

1. Open **RPDev Feed** from your app drawer.
2. Tap **Settings &gt; Hub Modules &amp; Cards**.
3. You will see a list of all your active cards.
4. Use the **Up/Down arrow buttons** (or drag the handle) to move cards up or down.
5. Your custom order is applied immediately!

---

## 👁️ Hiding or Showing Cards

- In **Settings &gt; Hub Modules &amp; Cards**, toggle the switch next to any card to turn it **ON** or **OFF**.
- Hidden cards stop making background network requests, saving battery and data.

---

## 🎨 Display Modes & Themes

RPDev Feed adapts to your visual preference:

- **Compact Cards**: Shows concise summary rows instead of large preview images so you can see more cards at once.
- **Pure AMOLED Black**: Saves battery on OLED screens by turning card backgrounds completely pitch black.
- **Dynamic Color (Material You)**: Harmonizes card accent colors with your Android wallpaper.
- **Card Transparency**: Adjust the transparency slider to let your wallpaper peek through the feed cards.
