## Purpose
This policy ensures the continuity of operations during infectious disease outbreaks, severe climate events, geopolitical instability, or any event causing widespread geographical disruption. It establishes a permanent, secure, and resilient hybrid-work framework.

## Permanent Hybrid-Work and Edge-Compute Realities
The organization operates under a "remote-first, hybrid-ready" 2026 model.
- **Edge Computing:** Critical workloads are distributed across edge compute nodes to ensure low-latency access for a globally distributed workforce, even if centralized regional hubs experience outages.
- **Zero-Trust Security:** Physical office networks are treated with the same zero-trust principles as public coffee shop networks. All devices are authenticated and continuously authorized.
- **Hardware Agnosticism:** Employees are provisioned with secure, cloud-based virtual workspaces accessible from any approved device via FIDO2 biometric authentication.

## Disruption Response Tiers

### Tier 1: Localized Disruption (e.g., Regional Weather Event)
- **Action:** Affected employees transparently shift to 100% remote work. Edge compute nodes automatically reroute traffic from the affected region.
- **Operations:** Minimal to no impact.

### Tier 2: Broad Disruption (e.g., National Health Emergency)
- **Action:** Mandatory remote work for all non-essential facility staff. Cloud infrastructure scaling triggered to support 100% concurrent remote connections.
- **Operations:** Asynchronous communication prioritized to accommodate disrupted employee schedules.

### Tier 3: Global Disruption (e.g., Global Pandemic or Coordinated Cyber-Kinetic Event)
- **Action:** Activation of full Business Continuity Plans. Supply chain contingencies activated for hardware procurement.
- **Operations:** Core services prioritized. AI-assisted automated workflows compensate for reduced human workforce availability.

## Employee Well-being and Support
In the event of extended disruptions, the organization provides support for remote ergonomics, mental health resources, and flexible asynchronous working hours to accommodate caregiving and personal resilience.
