# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

Welcome to the RPDev ecosystem! This guide will walk you through downloading, installing, and connecting **RPDev Launcher** and **RPDev Feed** in just a few minutes.

<div class="step-list">

  <div class="step-item">
    <div class="step-num">1</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Download the APK Files</h4>
      <p>Download the latest stable release APKs to your Android phone from our verified GitHub releases:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>RPDev Launcher</strong>: <a href="https://github.com/RPDevs-Builds/RPDev-Launcher/releases/tag/v1.2-beta">Download RPDev Launcher v1.2-beta</a></li>
        <li><strong>RPDev Feed</strong>: <a href="https://github.com/RPDevs-Builds/RPDev-Feed/releases/tag/v1.2-beta">Download RPDev Feed v1.2-beta</a></li>
      </ul>
      <p><em>Tip: Both apps work seamlessly together, but you can also install RPDev Launcher on its own.</em></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step-item">
    <div class="step-num">2</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Allow Installation from Your Browser / Files</h4>
      <p>When you tap on the downloaded <code>.apk</code> file in your Downloads folder, Android may prompt: <em>"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."</em></p>
      <p>Tap <strong>Settings</strong> on the pop-up and toggle <strong>"Allow from this source"</strong> to ON. Then tap <strong>Install</strong>.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step-item">
    <div class="step-num">3</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Set RPDev Launcher as Your Default Home Screen</h4>
      <p>Once installed, press your phone's <strong>Home button</strong> or swipe up from the bottom:</p>
      <ol>
        <li>A pop-up will appear asking which app to use as Home.</li>
        <li>Select <strong>RPDev Launcher</strong>.</li>
        <li>Tap <strong>"Always"</strong>.</li>
      </ol>
      <p><em>(You can always change this back at any time in your phone's Android Settings &gt; Apps &gt; Default Apps &gt; Home app).</em></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step-item">
    <div class="step-num">4</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h4>Connect the -1 Screen Feed</h4>
      <p>To enable the swipe-left Google Discover alternative:</p>
      <ol>
        <li>Long-press on any empty spot on your home screen.</li>
        <li>Tap <strong>Home settings</strong> (or <strong>Launcher Settings</strong>).</li>
        <li>Tap <strong>Search &amp; Feed</strong>.</li>
        <li>Turn ON <strong>"Enable Feed Overlay"</strong>.</li>
        <li>Under <strong>"Feed Provider"</strong>, select <strong>RPDev Feed</strong>.</li>
      </ol>
      <p>Now return to your home screen and swipe all the way to the left! Your sovereign feed is live.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

</div>
